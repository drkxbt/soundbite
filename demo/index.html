soundbite

The best minute of your Space, as a video for X

Open the recording, drag over the moment, and the words are written out and timed as they are said. Pick a look and download an MP4. Everything runs on this device, so the audio is never uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Open the recording. Drop an MP3, M4A or WAV on the editor, or a video for its sound, or open one with the folder key under the picture.
  2. Pick the moment. Drag the green edges on the waveform. A clip runs up to 2:20, the longest most X accounts can post. To join several moments, press the add key after each one: they play back to back, and you can drag their chips into another order.
  3. Check the words. The captions are written as the recording opens. Fix any word in the captions list; for another part, move the edges and press the mic key.
  4. Make it yours. Your name, handle and title, a profile picture, a background picture and a logo, then a shape and colours. Add music under your voice with the note key and set its level with the slider. All of it but the music is remembered for next time.
  5. Download the MP4. Post it to X as a video. The captions are part of the picture, so they show with the sound off.

Good to know

  • The recording, the words and your pictures stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • The speech model, 44 to 66 MB, comes from Hugging Face the first time and is kept by the browser after that.
  • Square suits the feed, 4:5 fills more of a phone, and 16:9 fits YouTube or LinkedIn.
  • Use music you're allowed to share. Platforms take down or mute clips with songs they don't have the rights to.
  • Every clip carries a small "made with drk.vc/soundbite" in its corner. The key at the right of the bar takes it off the next download.
  • Soundbite is free and open source: the code is on GitHub.
